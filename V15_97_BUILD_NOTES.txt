Nickname policy:
- Initial nickname assignment: free.
- First nickname change: free.
- Third nickname selection and every later change: 500 Coin.
- 30-day cooldown between nickname changes.
- Current Monthly Leaderboard Top 5 cannot change nickname until the month closes.
- Monthly winner nickname remains independent of later nickname changes when archived server-side.

Game exit policy:
- Solo exit confirmation: "أفا متأكد طالع/ه ؟👀" + lose one whole heart.

- Daily exit NEVER deducts 250 Coin from the player's existing balance.

- On early Daily exit, earned challenge Coins are retained; zero correct answers means zero earned Coin.
- Daily challenge becomes used for the day after confirmed/abandoned exit.
- Active sessions persist so page refresh/app closure cannot bypass the exit consequence.

Existing V.15.96 hardening features retained, including Automatic Player Sync visible.

Timer behavior:
- The active 15-second question countdown continues while the exit-confirmation modal is open.

- Choosing 'إيه' stops the timer only when the player actually exits.
